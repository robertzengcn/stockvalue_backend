      - **positive_cfo**: Test with operating_cash_flow=200 (> 0 -> score 1);
        then operating_cash_flow=-100 (< 0 -> score 0).
      - **improving_roa**: Test with current ROA > previous ROA (score 1);

      - **higher_liquidity**: cash/liabilities increases year-over-year (score 1);
        decreases (score 0).
      - **no_new_shares**: shares_outstanding stays same or decreases (score 1);
        increases (score 0).
      - **improving_margin**: gross_margin increases (score 1); decreases (score 0).
      - **improving_turnover**: revenue/assets increases (score 1); decreases (score 0).
